# 일일매출수금현황 (Branch Daily Sales & Collections)

Per-branch daily report. Each branch fills this in daily. Feeds into the 일보현황.

## Section 1: 판매현황 (Sales Status)

**Purpose:** Daily sales broken down by product brand/line. Shows what the branch sold today and how the month is tracking.

**Rows (by brand):**
- Mobil
- Mobil-MB
- 블라자 (Blazer)
- 훅스 (Fuchs)
- 기타 (Other — Shell and other competitor products)

**Columns:**
- 전일누계 — Previous day cumulative (month-to-date as of yesterday)
- 당일 — Today's sales
- 누계 — New cumulative (month-to-date including today)
- 비고 — Remarks

## Section 2: 수금현황 (Collections Status)

**Purpose:** How much money the branch collected today, with totals.

**Rows:**
- 매출액 — Sales amount
- 수금액 broken down by:
  - Cash — 현금
  - 어음 — Notes/bills
  - 카드 — Card
  - 합계 — Total collections

## Section 3: 매입/발주 (Purchases / Orders)

**Purpose:** Mobil purchasing activity and IL Flagship product tracking — both what was bought and what was sold.

**Metrics:**
- 매입/발주 모빌
  - Vol(L) — Mobil purchase volume in liters
  - 금액 — Amount (monetary value)
- IL (Flagship) 매출
  - Vol(L) — Flagship product sold (sell-out volume)
- IL (Flagship) 매입
  - Vol(L) — Flagship product purchased (sell-in volume)

## Section 4: 재고 (Inventory)

**Purpose:** Daily inventory movement by product brand. Shows stock flow: what we had, what came in, what went out, what's left.

**Rows (by brand):**
- Mobil
- Mobil-MB
- 블라자 (Blazer)
- 훅스 (Fuchs)
- 기타 (Other — Shell and other competitor products)

**Columns:**
- 전일재고 — Previous day inventory
- 입고 — Incoming stock (received)
- 출고 — Outgoing stock (shipped/sold)
- 재고 — Current inventory

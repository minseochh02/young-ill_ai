# 장기미수금현황 (Long-term AR Outstanding)

Monthly review of overdue receivables.

## Sheet Structure

**Tabs:**
- **Current month summary** — Company-wide overview with 3 sections
- **7 branch tabs:** 화성, 창원, 부산, 동부, 서부, 중부, 제주 — Detailed customer-level aging

## Current Month Summary Tab

### Section 1: <{Year} {Month} 기준 미수금 현황> (AR Status as of Month)

**Purpose:** Branch-level summary showing total AR, long-term AR ratios, and month-over-month comparison.

**Columns:**
- 사업소 — Branch
- 당월 총 미수금 — Current month total AR
- 3개월 이상 장기미수금 — 3+ months long-term AR
- 장기미수금 비율 — Long-term AR ratio (%)
- 전월 장기미수 — Previous month long-term AR
- 전월대비 장기미수 — Long-term AR vs previous month

### Section 2: <3개월 이상 장기미수 업체의 전체 미수금 현황> (Total AR for Companies with 3+ Months Overdue)

**Purpose:** Focuses on the total receivables from companies that have overdue balances of 3 months or more.

**Columns:**
- 사업소 — Branch
- 당월 장기미수금 — Current month long-term AR
- 전월 장기미수 — Previous month long-term AR
- 전월대비 장기미수 — Long-term AR vs previous month
- 비고 — Remarks

### Section 3: <미수 5개월 이상 출고 업체 현황> (Companies with 5+ Months Overdue Shipments)

**Purpose:** Lists company names with severely overdue receivables (5+ months), grouped by branch.

**Branches shown:** 화성, 창원, 부산, 동부, 서부, 중부, 제주

**Data:** Company names for each branch

## Branch Tabs (Individual)

**Purpose:** Detailed customer-level accounts receivable aging with monthly breakdown. Shows exactly which customers owe money, how long it's been overdue, and tracks changes over time.

**Header format:** {branch}영업소 3개월이상 장기미수 / {start date} ~ {end date} / {as of date} 기준
- Example: 창원영업소 3개월이상 장기미수 / 2024/08/01 ~ 2025/07/31 / 2025.07.31 기준

**Columns:**
- 담당자 — Manager/Person in charge
- 거래처코드 — Customer Code
- 거래처명 — Customer Name
- 구분 — Category/Classification
- 이월잔액 — Carried-over Balance
- Monthly columns: 2024/08 ~ 2025/07 (12 months of aging detail)
- 잔액 — Current Balance
- 처리방안 — Action Plan/Handling Method

The monthly breakdown allows management to see exactly when receivables became overdue and track collection progress month by month. The 처리방안 column documents the planned approach for each overdue account (e.g., payment plan, legal action, write-off consideration).

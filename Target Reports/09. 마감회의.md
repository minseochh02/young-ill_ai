# 마감회의 (Closing Meeting Report)

End-of-month summary for management meeting. Aggregates everything above.

## Sheet Structure

**Tabs:**
- **실적** — Actual results (past year data)
- **계획** — Plan (current year targets and projections)

## Structure

**Purpose:** Comprehensive month-end performance tracking across all business segments, comparing actual results against targets and previous year performance. Shows both cumulative and monthly breakdowns.

**Column structure:**
- 구분 — Category/Classification (hierarchical rows)
- 2023년 실적 — 2023 Actual Results
- 2024년 실적 — 2024 Actual Results
- 2025년(P) — 2025 Plan/Projection
- 증감율 — Change Rate
- 누적 — Cumulative (with sub-columns)
- Monthly columns: 1월 ~ 10월 (January through October, each with sub-columns)

**Sub-columns for 누적 and each month:**
- 2024년 — 2024 actual
- 목표 — Target/Goal (2025)
- 2025년 — 2025 actual
- 달성율 — Achievement rate (%)
- 전년대비 — Compared to previous year

This report provides a complete picture of company performance by segment, team, and branch, enabling management to assess which areas are meeting targets and which need attention. The multi-year comparison (2023, 2024, 2025) and month-by-month tracking allow for trend analysis and early identification of performance gaps.

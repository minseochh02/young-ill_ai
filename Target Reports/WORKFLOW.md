# Report Generation Workflow

The logical order a manager would produce these reports, from daily operations up to strategic analysis.

## Daily (every working day)

1. **영일오엔씨 일보현황 2025년** — Daily report (일보)
   - First thing every morning. Sales, collections, AR balance, funds for all branches.
   - Everything downstream depends on this.

2. **(창원) 일일매출수금 현황-202511** — Branch daily sales/collections
   - Each branch fills in their own daily numbers. Feeds into the 일보.

3. **1. 화성 발주서J** — Purchase orders
   - Daily operational task. Branch submits orders as needed.

## Weekly / As-needed

4. **재고파악시트(2025년11월)** — Inventory tracking
   - Regular stock checks to keep inventory accurate.

5. **사업소별 장기재고 현황_2025.10** — Long-term inventory by branch
   - Flags slow-moving stock. Reviewed weekly or before month-end.

## Monthly

6. **1~12월 판매현황 지선8.20** — Full-year sales status
   - Monthly accumulation of sales data across the year.

7. **2025 11월 B2B사업소 일일매출 분석** — B2B daily sales analysis
   - Monthly analysis of B2B branch performance from daily data.

8. **미거래업체 현황_25.07** — Inactive customer status
   - Monthly review of customers with no transactions.

9. **장기미수금 현황_25.07** — Long-term AR outstanding
   - Monthly review of overdue receivables.

## Month-end closing

10. **25.10 마감회의 / 25.10 마감회의(1)** — Closing meeting report
    - End-of-month summary for management meeting. Aggregates everything above.

11. **B2C 회의자료 2025년 10월** — B2C meeting materials
    - Companion to closing meeting, focused on B2C segment.

12. **B2B 2024년 이후 자료** — B2B historical data
    - Reference data for trend comparison during closing meetings.

# 미거래업체현황 (Inactive Customer Status)

Monthly review of customers with no transactions.

## Sheet Structure

**Tabs (by branch):**
- 화성 미거래업체
- 창원 미거래업체
- 부산 미거래업체
- 동부 미거래업체
- 서부 미거래업체
- 중부 미거래업체
- 제주 미거래업체

## Data Structure

**Purpose:** Identifies customers who have not made any transactions within a 3-month period. Helps management identify at-risk accounts, evaluate customer retention, and plan re-engagement strategies.

**Header format:** {branch}영업소 3개월 미거래 업체 / {start date} ~ {end date}
- Example: 화성영업소 3개월 미거래 업체 / 2025/05/01 ~ 2025/07/31

**Columns:**
- 담당자 — Manager/Person in charge
- 거래처코드 — Customer Code
- 거래처명 — Customer Name
- 구분 — Category/Classification
- 잔액 — Balance (outstanding balance if any)
- 처리방안 — Action Plan/Handling Method

Each branch tab shows customers who have been inactive for 3 months within that branch's territory. The 처리방안 column allows managers to document their planned approach for each inactive customer (e.g., follow-up call, account closure, special promotion, etc.).

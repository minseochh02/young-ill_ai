## Tab 1: 자료 기준 (Data Standard/Baseline)

**Purpose:** Reference documentation explaining data organization standards, rules, and historical adjustments applied across all tabs.

**Content:** Text-based reference guide, not a data table.

### 자료 정리 기준 (Data Organization Standards)

1. **기본 기준 (Basic Standard):** Data is organized based on customer registration information in the ERP system (담당자, 업종, 지역 등).

2. **가상업체 등록 (Virtual Company Registration):** For MRO companies or cases where companies sell to specific customers/industries, virtual companies are registered separately in the system to match actual transactions. (2022-2023 data was organized based on voucher description content)

3. **2024년 이후 (From 2024):** Except for some January entries, items 1-2 above are recorded in vouchers and created based on the ERP system. Sales reps who want to apply different classification than the ERP must request virtual company registration from the management support team during sales.

4. **B2C 산업유 (B2C Industrial Oil):** Industrial oil products sold in the B2C segment - only Mobil industrial oil products are separately added.

5. **Historical Data Adjustments:**
   - **2022:** Included sales from 중앙, excluded sales from 영일 to 중앙, excluded duplicate 현대중공업/삼광엔씨 transactions (deleted 현대중공업 data)
   - **2023:** Excluded duplicate 현대중공업/삼광오엔씨 transactions (deleted 현대중공업 data)
   - **2024:** Excluded duplicate 현대중공업/삼광오엔씨 transactions (deleted 현대중공업 data)

**Important Note:** If customer registration information needs correction, request modifications from the management support team. When data is corrected in the ERP, historical data is automatically updated as well.

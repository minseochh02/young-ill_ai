# 일보현황 (Daily Report)

Daily operational report covering all branches. 6 sections.

## Section 1: 매출현황 (Sales Status)

**Purpose:** Sales performance at two time scales.

**일계 (Daily):** What each branch sold today.
- Total revenue across all products
- Mobil sell-out: how much Mobil product was sold to end customers — the monetary value, total volume in liters, and how much of that volume was flagship (premium) products
- Mobil sell-in: how much Mobil product the branch received from the supplier — total volume and flagship volume

**월누계 (Monthly Cumulative):** Same metrics accumulated from the 1st of the month through today.

**Per branch:** Each of the 8 branches reports independently so management can compare branch performance side by side.

The sell-out vs sell-in distinction matters because it shows whether branches are selling through their Mobil inventory (sell-out) at the same pace they're restocking (sell-in). Flagship tracking lets management see if branches are pushing premium products.

Both halves share the same columns:

**Metrics per half:**
- 총매출액 — Total sales amount
- 모빌 Sell-out — Mobil sold to end customers
  - 모빌금액 (amount)
  - Total (L) (volume in liters)
  - Flagship (L) (flagship product volume)
- 모빌 Sell-in — Mobil received from supplier
  - Total (L)
  - Flagship (L)

**Rows (by branch):** 서울/화성IL, 창원, 화성auto(남부), 화성auto(중부), 인천(서부), 남양주(동부), 제주, 부산

## Section 2: 외상매출금 현황 (AR / Collections Status via 입금보고서)

**Purpose:** Collections and accounts receivable status at two time scales plus a balance snapshot.

**일계 (Daily):** What each branch collected today, broken down by payment method — total collections, cash, notes/bills, card, and other. Shows how money is actually coming in.

**누계 (Monthly Cumulative):** Same collection metrics accumulated from the 1st of the month through today.

**외상매출금 (AR Balance):** The current receivables position per branch — previous month's outstanding balance, current month's sales added on top, and the resulting current balance. Tells management how much customers still owe.

**Per branch:** Each of the 8 branches reports independently. The payment method breakdown matters because cash is immediately available, cards have processing delays, and notes/bills have maturity dates — so the mix affects actual liquidity.

Three parts sharing these columns:

**일계 / 누계 metrics (mirrored):**
- 총수금액 — Total collections
- 현금 — Cash
- 어음 — Notes/bills
- 카드 — Card
- 기타 (x2) — Other (two columns)

**외상매출금 (AR balance):**
- 전월잔액 — Previous month balance
- 당월매출 — Current month sales
- 현 잔액 — Current balance

**Rows (by branch):** 화성IL, 창원, 화성auto(남부), 화성auto(중부), 인천(서부), 남양주(동부), 제주, 부산

## Section 3: 자금 현황 (Funds Status)

**Purpose:** The company's full financial position across all accounts and instruments.

Unlike sections 1-2 which are per branch, this is company-wide. For each account type, it shows four states:
- 전잔 — balance carried from previous day
- 당입 — money coming in today
- 지출 — money going out today
- 현잔 — resulting current balance

This gives management a daily snapshot of total liquidity — how much cash is accessible, what's tied up in notes and deposits, foreign currency exposure, and how much is owed in short/long-term borrowings. The 전잔→당입→지출→현잔 flow makes it easy to see what changed today and why.

**Columns:**
- 보통예금 — General deposit
- 어음
  - 전자어음 — Electronic notes
  - 외담대 — External loans
  - 받을어음 — Notes receivable
- 적금+보험 — Savings + insurance
- CMA (미래에셋, 대신증권)
- 외화정기예금 — Foreign currency time deposits
- 외화예금 — Foreign currency deposits (USD, EUR, JPY)
- 외화보통예금 — Foreign currency general deposit (USD)
- 한도대출잔액 — Credit limit loan balance
- 단기차입금 — Short-term borrowings
- 장기차입금 — Long-term borrowings
- 퇴직연금 — Retirement pension
- 미결제어음 — Unsettled notes

## Section 4: 주요입금현황 (Major Deposits/Payments)

**Purpose:** Today's notable incoming deposits and outgoing payments, categorized by payment type (카드, 어음, 현금).

For each transaction it logs who paid us (입금처) or who we paid (지출처), the amount, and a description. The payment type categorization lets management quickly see the nature of cash flow — card payments have fees and delays, notes have maturity risk, cash is immediate. Essentially a daily ledger of significant money movements in and out.

**Metrics:**
- 입금처 — Deposit source (who paid us)
- 금액 — Amount
- Description — Notes/remarks
- 지출처 — Payment destination (who we paid)

Each row is categorized by payment type: 카드, 어음, 현금

## Section 5: 주요비용 지출현황 (Major Expense Disbursements)

**Purpose:** Significant expense disbursements for the day, categorized by payment type (카드, 어음, 현금).

While section 4 tracks both incoming and outgoing money, section 5 focuses specifically on costs — operational expenses, vendor payments, overhead, etc. Each entry has the amount and a description of what the expense was for. The payment type categorization shows how expenses were settled. This gives management visibility into where money is being spent daily, separate from routine collections and sales-related flows.

**Metrics:**
- 금액 — Amount
- Description — Expense description/remarks

Each row is categorized by payment type: 카드, 어음, 현금

## Section 6: 모빌결제내역 (Mobil Payment Details)

**Purpose:** Tracks 영일오엔씨's supplier debt to Mobil (ExxonMobil) per branch, broken down by product line.

영일오엔씨 is a distributor that buys Mobil products on credit and resells them. Each branch receives and sells those products. This section tracks how much each branch's purchases from Mobil have been paid off.

- 전일잔액 = what 영일오엔씨 owed Mobil yesterday
- 결제금액 = what 영일오엔씨 paid Mobil today
- 잔액 = what 영일오엔씨 still owes Mobil

The per-branch breakdown shows which branch is responsible for how much of that supplier debt. 일계 (daily) and 누계 (monthly cumulative) show payment activity over both time scales.

> **Note:** This data should come from the ERP system, but some report that it also comes in via Nateon mail service. To see how they come in, check REF. 발주서.

**Top-level:**
- 전일잔액 — Previous day balance
- 결제금액 — Payment amount

**Columns:**
- 사무소/구분 — Branch/division
- IL — Industrial Lubricants
- AUTO — Automotive
- MBK — Mobil Business Kit

Two halves: 일계 (daily) | 누계 (monthly cumulative)

**Rows (branches):**
- 화성 IL
- 창원 IL
- 화성 AUTO (중부)
- 남부지사
- 인천(서부)
- 남양주(동부)
- 제주
- 부산
- Total
- 잔액 (remaining balance)

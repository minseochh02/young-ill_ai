# B2B일일매출분석 (B2B Daily Sales Analysis)

Monthly analysis of B2B branch performance from daily data.

## Structure

**Purpose:** Daily overview of all branches' B2B sales performance, showing profitability analysis including purchase costs, sales amounts, and profit margins calculated using different pricing methods (DSP/ASP).

**Date format:** YYYY-MM-DD (요일) — e.g., 2025-11-13 (목)

**Branches covered:** All 사업소 (branches) shown together for daily comparison

## Key Metrics

**일일매출 현황 (Daily Sales Status)**
- 수량 — Quantity
- 단가 — Unit Price
- 공급가 — Supply Price

**구매현황 (Purchase Status)**
- Purchase-related costs and volumes

**매출이익 (Sales Profit)**
- DSP — Distributor Selling Price basis
- ASP — Actual Selling Price basis
- 기타비용(운임 외) — Other costs (shipping, etc.)
- 매출이익(DSP) — Sales Profit calculated using DSP
- 매출이익(ASP) — Sales Profit calculated using ASP

**이익율 (%) (Profit Rate %)**
- 이익률(DSP) — Profit Rate using DSP
- 이익율(ASP) — Profit Rate using ASP

**비고 (Remarks)**
- Additional notes

This report allows management to quickly compare daily B2B performance across all branches, seeing both gross sales and net profitability after accounting for purchase costs and other expenses. The dual pricing analysis (DSP vs ASP) helps track margin differences between list price and actual selling price.

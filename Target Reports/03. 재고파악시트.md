# 재고파악시트 (Inventory Tracking)

Regular stock checks to keep inventory accurate. Tracked weekly or as-needed.

**Tracking frequency:** Daily inventory tracking with totals shown for each day. Total summary provided at the end of each month.

## Sheet Structure

**Tabs:**
- **all** — Combined data across all branches
- Individual branch tabs:
  - 화성IL
  - 창원
  - 화성auto(남부)
  - 화성auto(중부)
  - 인천(서부)
  - 남양주(동부)
  - 제주
  - 부산

## Data Structure

**Purpose:** Detailed inventory tracking by product division and type. Shows the full inventory lifecycle: what we started with, what we bought, what we sold, transfers between locations, and what remains.

**Unit:** All values in liters (L)

**Note:** Each branch tab uses the same row structure below. The "all" tab aggregates data across all branches, while individual branch tabs show branch-specific inventory movements.

**Product divisions:**
- **Auto** — Automotive products (has Flagship and Others)
- **IL** — Industrial Lubricants (has Flagship and Others)
- **MB** — Mobil Business (has Others only)

**Product types:**
- **Flagship** — Premium/flagship products
- **Others** — Standard/other products

This structure allows management to track inventory flow for each product division and quality tier separately, helping identify which product lines are moving fastest and where stock adjustments are needed.

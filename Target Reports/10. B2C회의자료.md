# B2C회의자료 (B2C Meeting Materials)

Companion to closing meeting, focused on B2C segment.

## Sheet Structure

**Tabs:**
1. 사업소별 — By branch
2. 21 담당자별매출 — By person in charge sales (21)
3. 4 매출액 — Sales amount (4)
4. 매출분석-채널 — Sales analysis - Channel
5. 거래처별원인 — By customer cause/reason
6. 신규 — New
7. 55 제품별현황 — Product status (55)
8. 4 팀밋전략딜러 — Team MIT strategy dealer (4)
9. 팀물량 — Team volume
10. 팀매출액 — Team sales amount
11. 쇼핑몰판매현황 — Shopping mall sales status

## Tab 1: 사업소별 (By Branch)

**Title:** AUTO 판매 실적 총괄 (AUTO Sales Performance Summary)

**Unit:** 리터 (Liters)

**Purpose:** Summary of B2C AUTO sales performance by branch, showing year-over-year comparison and monthly tracking against targets.

**Column structure:**
- 구분 — Category
- 2023년 실적 — 2023 Actual
- 2024년 실적 — 2024 Actual
- 2025년 계획 — 2025 Plan
- 증감율 — Change Rate (yearly)
- 누적 — Cumulative (with sub-columns)
- Monthly: 1월 ~ 10월 (January through October, each with sub-columns)

**Sub-columns for 누적 and months:**
- 2024년 — 2024 actual
- 목표 — Target
- 2025년 — 2025 actual
- 달성율 — Achievement rate
- 증감율 — Change rate

**Rows:**
- 합계 — Total
- 동부사업소 — East branch
- 서부사업소 — West branch
- 중부사업소 — Central branch
- 제주사업소 — Jeju branch
- 남부지사 — South branch
- B2B본부 — B2B headquarters

## Tab 2: 21 담당자별매출 (By Person in Charge Sales)

**Notes:**
- 사무실은 전부 딜러로 함. — All offices are classified as dealers
- 개인의 기타부분은 lcc로 분류 — Individual "other" sections are classified as LCC

**Unit:** 리터 (Liters)

**Purpose:** Detailed sales performance by individual sales representatives, broken down by customer type (Fleet vs LCC).

**Column structure:**
- 구분 (PVL, CVL) — Category (product type)
- 전체 거래처수 — Total customer count
- 거래처수 — Customer count
- 2023년 실적 — 2023 Actual
- 2024년 실적 — 2024 Actual
- 2025년 계획 — 2025 Plan
- 증감율 — Change Rate
- 누적실적 — Cumulative results (with sub-columns)
- Monthly: 1월 ~ 10월 (each with sub-columns)

**Sub-columns for 누적실적 and months:**
- 2024년, 목표, 2025년, 달성율, 증감율

**Rows (hierarchical structure):**
- **Team level** (e.g., 맥심팀)
  - 합계 — Team total
  - **Individual sales rep** (e.g., 심경섭, 이상욱)
    - 소계 — Individual subtotal
    - Fleet — Fleet customers
    - LCC — LCC customers

This structure repeats for all B2C teams.

**Sub-columns for 누적실적 and months:**
- 2024년, 목표, 2025년, 달성율, 증감율

**Rows (hierarchical structure):**

## Tab 4: 매출분석-채널 (Sales Analysis - Channel)

**Title:** 매출분석(2024 VS 2025년) — Sales Analysis (2024 VS 2025)

**Unit:** 리터 (Liters)

**Purpose:** Year-over-year comparison of sales by product type (CVL vs PVL), with cause analysis and action plans for significant changes.

**Columns:**
- 구분 2024년 01월~10월 — Category 2024 Jan~Oct
  - CVL
  - PVL
  - Total
- 구분 2025년 01월~10월 — Category 2025 Jan~Oct
  - CVL
  - PVL
  - Total
- CVL — CVL comparison
- PVL — PVL comparison
- 증감 — Change (volume)
- 증감율 — Change rate (%)
- 원인분석 — Cause analysis
- 대처방안 — Action plan

## Tab 5: 거래처별원인 (By Customer Cause/Reason)

**Summary row:** 합계 : 중량 (Total: Weight) — Shows cumulative totals

**Purpose:** Customer-level analysis of sales changes between years, identifying specific accounts with significant increases or decreases and documenting the reasons and planned responses.

**Columns:**
- 거래처그룹2 — Customer Group 2
- 담당자명 — Manager Name
- 거래처코드 — Customer Code
- 판매처명 — Sales Customer Name
- 2024년 — 2024 (volume)
- 2025년 — 2025 (volume)
- 증감(L) — Change (Liters)
- 원인분석 — Cause analysis
- 대응방안 — Action plan/Response plan

This detailed customer-level view allows management to understand exactly which accounts are driving changes in overall sales performance and what actions are being taken to address losses or capitalize on gains.

## Tab 6: 신규 (New)

**Title:** 신규거래처 (물량:워셔액제외) — New Customers (volume: excluding washer fluid)

**Unit:** 리터 / 공급가액 (Liters / Supply amount)

**Purpose:** Tracks new customer acquisition, showing how many new customers were added, their purchase volumes, and revenue contribution.

**Column structure:**
- 구분 (물량:워셔액제외) — Category (volume: excluding washer fluid)
- 2024년 거래처수 — 2024 customer count
- 목표 거래처수 — Target customer count
- 물량 2024년 실적 — Volume 2024 actual
- 전체공급금액 2024년 실적 — Total supply amount 2024 actual
- 물량 2025년 계획 — Volume 2025 plan
- 증감율 — Change rate
- 금액 — Amount
- 거래처평균 — Average per customer
- 누적실적 — Cumulative results
  - 물량 — Volume
  - 금액 — Amount
  - 달성율 — Achievement rate
- Monthly: 1월 ~ 10월 (each with sub-columns)

**Sub-columns for each month:**
- 거래처수 — Customer count
- 물량 — Volume
- 금액 — Amount

**Rows (hierarchical structure):**
- **Team level** (e.g., 맥심팀)
  - 합계 — Team total
  - **Individual sales reps** (e.g., 심경섭, 이상욱)
    - 소계 — Individual subtotal

This report helps management evaluate new customer development efforts and track whether new accounts are meeting volume and revenue targets.

## Tab 7: 55 제품별현황 (Product Status)

**Title:** Product name (e.g., Auto) — Separate section for each product

**Unit:** KL (킬로리터 - Kiloliters)

**Purpose:** Product-specific performance tracking by team and sales rep, showing quarterly (Q4) results against targets and year-over-year comparison.

**Columns:**
- 구분 — Category (teams/sales reps)
- 2025년 목표 — 2025 Target
- 2024년 실적 — 2024 Actual
- 2025년 4/4분기 — 2025 Q4
  - 목표 — Target
  - 실적 — Actual
  - 달성율 — Achievement rate
  - 전년실적 — Previous year actual
  - 전년 대비 증감율 — Change rate vs previous year
- 2025년 4/4분기 목표 — 2025 Q4 Target

**Rows:** By teams and individual sales reps (hierarchical structure similar to other tabs)

**Products:** Multiple products shown (Auto and others)

This tab allows management to see which teams and individuals are performing well for specific product lines, helping identify product-specific sales strengths and weaknesses.

## Tab 8: 4 팀밋전략딜러 (Team MIT Strategy Dealer)

**Sections:**
- **PV/CV** — PV/CV segment
- **남부지사** — South branch
- **전략딜러** — Strategic dealers

**Purpose:** Detailed monthly tracking of MIT (strategic) dealers across three years, with monthly averages and comparison metrics. Each section shows the same teams but focuses on different dealer segments.

**Column structure (same for all sections):**
- 행 레이블 / 판매처명 — Row label / Sales customer name
- **2023년:**
  - 합계 — Total
  - 월평균 — Monthly average
- **2024년:**
  - 1월 ~ 12월 — All 12 months
  - 합계 — Total
  - 월평균 — Monthly average
- **2025년:**
  - 1월 ~ 12월 — All 12 months
  - 합계 — Total
  - 월평균 — Monthly average
- 평균 대비 — Compared to average
- 동기 대비 — Compared to same period (year-over-year)

**Rows:** Team names (맥심팀, 솔개팀, 아리안팀, etc.)

This comprehensive monthly view allows management to track strategic dealer performance by team and segment over time, identify seasonal patterns, and compare current year performance against historical averages and same-period last year.

## Tab 11: 쇼핑몰판매현황 (Shopping Mall Sales Status)

**Columns:**
- 팀명 — Team name
- 비고 — Remarks
- 2023년 — 2023
- 평균 — Average (2023)
- 2024년 — 2024
- 평균 — Average (2024)

This comprehensive B2C meeting materials file provides management with detailed visibility across multiple dimensions: branch performance, individual sales rep performance, product mix, customer acquisition, strategic dealer tracking, team volumes and revenues, and e-commerce sales — enabling data-driven decision making for the B2C segment.
